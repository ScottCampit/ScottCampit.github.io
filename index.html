Founder · Scientist · AI Engineer · Bay Area → Austin

Scott E. Campit, PhD

I’m a Computational Biologist and Research Scientist at Toby, building AI-based, non-invasive diagnostic technologies that support early detection of cancer, Alzheimer’s, and other diseases.

I earned my PhD at the University of Michigan, where I simulated biological processes and built AI models of cancer metabolism from multi-omics data to identify new biomarkers and support drug discovery. My work has been published in peer-reviewed journals, including Nature Communications, Cancer & Metabolism, and Clinical & Experimental Metastasis. My academic research was funded by the NIH/NCI, the DOE, and private institutions.

Throughout my career, I’ve applied AI to real-world data and high-impact problems. I’ve built clinical AI/NLP models for Medicare reimbursement, led a technical team as Head of Technology building AI products in the life sciences, and, as a founder, helped companies with AI transformation, enablement, and implementation.

I’m an SF Bay Area transplant now living in Austin, TX. Feel free to reach out for coffee — I love talking about all things science, diagnostics, technology, and AI.
