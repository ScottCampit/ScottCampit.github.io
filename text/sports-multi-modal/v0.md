Helping athletes perform better and get injured less using their phone's camera

We built an AI system that watches how athletes move — through any phone or web camera — and tells them in plain language where they're at risk of injury and how to improve their performance. No wearables, no lab equipment needed.

What we delivered
Evaluated the best AI tools for reading human body movement accurately in real-world conditions
Gathered and annotated athlete footage across running and basketball to teach the AI what good and risky movement looks like
Developed custom algorithms that translate raw movement data into actionable scores for each sport
Shipped a cross-platform application where athletes can record themselves and get instant AI-powered feedback
