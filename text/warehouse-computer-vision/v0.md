Making warehouses safer with AI that sees what humans miss

We partnered with a warehouse operator to build an AI-powered safety system that watches for hazards in real time — things like workers in danger zones or missing safety gear — so teams can act before accidents happen.

What we delivered
Research & model selection
Evaluated the best AI vision tools for detecting warehouse hazards accurately
Data collection & labeling
Gathered and annotated warehouse footage so the AI could learn what "safe" vs. "unsafe" looks like
Custom model training
Ran experiments to fine-tune the AI until it could reliably spot safety issues in real conditions
Landing page
Built a clear, compelling web presence to communicate the product to stakeholders and customers
Live safety monitoring app
Deployed a working application that analyzes camera feeds and flags hazards in real time
