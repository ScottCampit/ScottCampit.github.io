Teaching AI to speak the language of your business — not just the internet's

Off-the-shelf AI models are trained on general data — they're good at a lot of things, but rarely great at the specific tasks a business actually needs. We specialize in adapting and fine-tuning language models on custom datasets so they understand your domain, follow your rules, and perform reliably on the work that matters to you.

What we delivered
Custom language data pipelines: Built systems to collect, clean, and structure text datasets for specific tasks — from identifying named entities to classifying documents to learning user preferences
Prompt & context engineering: Crafted the instructions and context given to AI models to consistently get the right outputs — often improving performance significantly before any training is needed
Efficient model fine-tuning: Used techniques like LoRA to adapt large language models to new tasks without the time and cost of full retraining — making custom AI practical for more projects
Benchmarking & evaluation: Designed custom metrics to measure how well models perform on new tasks — giving clients a clear, honest picture of what the AI can and can't do
Traditional NLP models: Where a large AI model is overkill, we train leaner, faster classical models — the right tool for the job, not the most expensive one
