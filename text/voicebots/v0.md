Testing how easily employees can be tricked over the phone — before real attackers do

We built an agentic voicebot that calls employees and poses as a trusted contact — mimicking the exact tactics real fraudsters use to extract sensitive information. Retailers use it to find out where their human vulnerabilities are and get a clear risk score before a real attack exposes them.

What we delivered
Built an agentic system that conducts live phone-based social engineering tests — carrying on natural conversations designed to probe employee awareness
Developed a scoring model that grades how vulnerable each retailer is based on how employees responded during the simulated attacks
Created a web application with a lifelike AI avatar that syncs in real time with the voicebot — making simulations more realistic and assessments easier to run
Designed unique caller identities and scripted scenarios tailored to each client — from IT support impersonation to vendor fraud
