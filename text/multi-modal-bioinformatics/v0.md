Helping biotech researchers find cancer signals hidden in mountains of biological data

We partnered with multiple biotech companies to accelerate cancer biomarker discovery — the process of finding biological clues that indicate whether someone has cancer, how it will behave, or how they'll respond to treatment. We built the data infrastructure and AI tools that let researchers move faster and trust their findings more.

What we delivered
Built systems to ingest, clean, and prepare complex biological datasets — from gene expression in individual cells to protein and metabolite measurements
Applied machine learning to identify patterns across genomic, proteomic, and metabolomic data that human analysis alone would miss
Built an AI system that cross-references findings against published research — saving researchers weeks of manual literature review
Designed charts and interactive plots that make complex biological findings interpretable for both scientists and non-technical stakeholders
